Privacy Policy

HUM - Your Music, Your Voice · Effective November 13, 2026

Your Voice While You Record

When you record, HUM sends the sound from your microphone to HUM's servers, which turn it into notes and send the notes back. The recording is held in memory only while it is being turned into notes, and is not stored afterwards, unless you have turned on "Help Improve HUM" (below).

"Help Improve HUM" (Off Unless You Turn It On)

"Help Improve HUM" is one switch in the app's menu, also offered in the question HUM asks after your third take. Turning it on does two things: it keeps your takes (this section) and it counts how you use the app (Usage statistics, below). Turning it off stops both.

While it is on, we keep the recording of each take you make, and of a press you cancel: the few seconds of room sound before you hum, your hum, what HUM was playing while you hummed over your tracks, the notes HUM found and the words it showed you. We also keep a few technical facts about how it was recorded, such as the sample rate and the microphone settings.

  • We use these recordings only to make HUM hear notes better, including in future versions of HUM.
  • They are stored privately, are not linked to your name, account or device, are never sold, and are deleted two years after they are made.
  • You can turn "Help Improve HUM" off at any time in the app's menu. Takes recorded after that are not kept.
  • To have kept recordings deleted, email us (see Contact) with the approximate dates and times of the takes. Because they are not linked to you, those details are how we find them.

Your Saved Hums

The hums you save in "My HUMs" are stored only on your device, and we cannot see them, unless you share one (below) or turn on Sync (below). Deleting a hum, or the app, removes it from your device. "Export HUMS" creates a file that you choose where to keep.

Sharing a Song With Friends

When you choose "Share with Friends", HUM uploads a copy of that song and gives you a link to send. The copy holds the song's notes (as HUM first heard them and as you edited them), its instruments, track names, volumes, mood and speed, and, if you chose one, the name you asked HUM to show your friends (you can leave it blank). It never holds your voice or any recording.

  • Anyone who has the link can open a copy of the song in HUM, and see on the link's page how many tracks it has, which instruments play them, and the name you chose, if you chose one.
  • We do not know who shared a song or who opens it: the copy is not linked to your device or to any account. A name you chose travels inside the copy and shows on its page; you can change or clear it in HUM's menu. We count how many songs are shared and opened, without the songs or their links.
  • A shared song stays available so its link keeps working. To have one removed, email us (see Contact) with its link, or use "Report this link" on the link's page.

Accounts (Only If You Sign In)

HUM works without an account. If you choose to sign in with Apple, HUM makes an account so that Sync reaches your other devices. HUM asks Apple for no email address and no name: it keeps only a scrambled form of the number Apple gives for you, which cannot be turned back into it.

The account holds an account number HUM makes up and the devices signed in to it (three at most: what kind each is and when it was last used). Nothing else is asked for.

"Sign Out" takes one device off the account. "Delete Account" erases the account, its sign-ins, its devices and every copy kept for it; the HUMs on your devices stay.

Sync Between Your Devices

Sync is off unless you turn it on, and needs an account (above). While it is on, a copy of each HUM in My HUMs (its notes, instruments, names and settings, never the recording of your voice or the line it draws) is kept in your account, so your other devices can show it and take your changes within seconds. When two devices change the same track, the newer change wins and the other is kept for 30 days so you can bring it back. Deleting a HUM removes its copy.

When you scan a computer's code to link it, HUM uses your phone's camera only to read that code. Nothing the camera sees is recorded, kept or sent anywhere.

Feedback You Send

If you use "Report Issues / Send Feedback" or "Request Feature" from the search, we receive what you write, the tags you pick, and a few facts that help us reproduce the problem: your device and browser type, your screen size, the time on your device, and the state of the app (for example the instrument in use and how many notes are open). Reports are anonymous and are kept as long as they help us fix HUM.

Usage Statistics (Part Of "Help Improve HUM")

While "Help Improve HUM" is on (above), the app counts how you use it: which screens and buttons you use, how each take goes (whether notes were found and how long they took), and whether a song was saved, sent or opened. With these counts the app sends a random number it made and keeps on your phone, replaced every three months and whenever you turn the switch off, so we can tell a returning phone from a new one without knowing whose it is.

  • Never included: your voice, your notes, any name, any song or link, your address.
  • How it is read: by HUM's maker, as totals and as one phone's visits in order.
  • It is never combined with kept recordings, shared songs or feedback, which are stored apart from it.
  • How long: the records themselves for 13 months, totals indefinitely.
  • How to stop: turn "Help Improve HUM" off in the menu. Turning it off also replaces the number, so the records made before can no longer be tied to your phone, or to anything after, by design.

Diagnostics

When something goes wrong on HUM's servers, they log what failed and where, with technical details such as the app version; those logs exclude your recordings, your notes, your IP address and any account or device identifier. For each take, HUM's servers also keep a short record of technical facts: whether notes were found, the take's length, how long it took to turn into notes on the servers and on your phone, how closely the two agreed, and the kind of device and audio output it was made with. It holds no audio and no address, and holds notes only for a take that "Help Improve HUM" keeps. It is not linked to you, and is deleted after 13 months. The app and its page send no error reports to anyone.

Notifications (Only If You Turn Them On)

If you turn on HUM's weekly reminder, your phone schedules it itself. Nothing about it is sent to us. Turn it off in HUM's menu or in your phone's Settings.

Service Providers

We use a small number of providers to run HUM. They process data only on our behalf. Apple distributes HUM through the App Store and TestFlight, and confirms who you are if you sign in. Cloudflare delivers the app's pages, protects and connects HUM's servers, and stores opted-in recordings, feedback, shared songs and usage statistics, and, if you sign in, your account and the copies Sync keeps. Fly.io runs HUM's servers. To deliver the service, these providers process the network address your device connects from. HUM itself does not store it. On hummusic.app, the Home and About pages use Whop's pixel to count visits to those pages; Whop receives what your browser sends when it loads them: the page, the browser's type and the address you connect from. The HUM app itself uses no such tool.

What We Don't Do

  • No account unless you choose to sign in, and HUM never asks for your email address or phone number.
  • No advertising, and the HUM app does not track you across other apps or websites.
  • No selling or renting of any data.

Security

Everything HUM sends travels encrypted (HTTPS and secure WebSockets). Kept recordings and feedback are stored in private storage that only HUM's servers and its developer can access. Shared songs are stored apart from them, and open only through their links. An account and its copies of your HUMs are kept apart from everything else, and open only to the devices signed in to it.

Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU and UK, or the CCPA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete data about you, or to object to its use. Email us and we will respond within 30 days. Apart from an account you chose to make, HUM collects nothing linked to your identity, so we may ask for details, such as the dates of your takes, that let us find what you are asking about. You can erase an account yourself with "Delete Account" in the app.

Children

HUM is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us recordings with "Help Improve HUM" on, email us and we will delete them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will post the new version here with a new effective date. If a change affects how recordings are used, we will also say so in the app.

Contact

Email creator@hummusic.app about privacy, data deletion or anything in this policy.
