The Free Version

HUM is free to use, within three limits:

  • 3 projects.
  • 15 tracks in each project.
  • Songs as long as the first 5 bars.

Nothing you make is deleted because of a limit. A hum that runs past the first 5 bars is kept whole: the part past bar 5 plays, and is locked until you have HUM Pro. A fourth project you hum is kept too: it plays and can be shared, and is locked until you delete another project or have HUM Pro. A bigger project a friend shares with you opens whole too: you can play all of it, edit the part inside the limits, and add one hummed track of your own.

Within those limits, humming, the grid and editing your notes, instruments, drums, sharing a song with friends and everything else not listed under HUM Pro below are free. The Chord Builder, Key Changer, Melody Maker, Sample Chopper, Arpeggiator, Tone and Reverb tools and the search suggestions that use them, exporting a song as an audio file, a MIDI file or a HUM project, syncing with a computer, dragging tracks into a DAW, and the In Key rows' labels for the root, third and fifth need HUM Pro.
