Official Rules

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. A PURCHASE DOES NOT IMPROVE YOUR CHANCES OF WINNING. Open only to legal residents of the 50 United States and the District of Columbia who are at least 18 years old and have reached the age of majority where they live. Void where prohibited.

The HUM Thanksgiving Beat Battle (the "Contest") is a contest of skill: judges pick the winners, not chance. By entering, you agree to these Official Rules and to the judges' decisions.

1. Organizer

The Contest is organized by Pranav Nair, 283 Flatbush Avenue Extension, Brooklyn, NY 11201, for HUM (the "Organizer"). There is no outside sponsor.

Send entries, claims and questions to pranavnair963@gmail.com.

2. No Purchase Necessary

HUM is free to download. You can make and send a beat with HUM's free version. You do not need HUM Pro, a subscription or a free trial, and you never pay to enter or to claim a prize.

Sending the link needs an internet connection. Your carrier's usual data rates may apply.

3. Who Can Enter

You can enter if you are a legal resident of one of the 50 United States or the District of Columbia, are at least 18 years old, and have reached the age of majority where you live.

You cannot enter if you are a member of the HUM team or a judge, or an immediate family member (spouse, partner, parent, child or sibling) or household member of a HUM team member or a judge.

These rules apply to Referrers too (Section 6). A Referrer must meet every rule in this section to receive a prize.

The Contest is void where prohibited or restricted by law.

4. Entry Period

The Contest opens Monday, November 23, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. Eastern Time (ET) and closes Saturday, November 28, 2026, at 11:59 p.m. ET (the "Entry Period"). Your entry must arrive in the Organizer's inbox during the Entry Period. The time the Organizer's email account shows is the Contest's official clock.

Winners are announced Wednesday, December 2, 2026.

5. How to Enter

Download HUM on an iPhone, iPad, Mac or Windows computer, using the links in How to Enter above.

Create an original beat that starts from your own hum, made entirely in HUM.

In HUM, tap Share, then Share with Friends, and send the link by email from your own email address to pranavnair963@gmail.com. In that email, give your full name. If someone referred you, also give their full name and email address (Section 6).

One entry per person. Sending more than one entry, or entering under more than one name or email address, can disqualify all of your entries.

An entry that is late, incomplete, or whose link does not open in HUM cannot win. The Organizer is not responsible for email that is lost, late, misdirected or filtered, or for problems with devices, networks or HUM's link service.

6. Referrals

In your original entry email, you can name one eligible person who referred you (your "Referrer"), with their full name and email address.

If your entry places first, second or third, your Referrer receives the same prize you do: the same cash amount and HUM Pro for life.

You cannot refer yourself. You cannot add or change a Referrer after you send your entry. A Referrer does not need to enter.

If your Referrer is not eligible, cannot be verified or does not claim in time, the Referrer's prize is not awarded. Your own prize does not change.

The most cash the Contest pays in total is $360.

7. Judging

The HUM team judges each eligible entry on three criteria, weighted equally: originality, musical quality, and creative use of HUM. The judges play each entry in HUM.

Ties are broken by the higher originality score, then by the higher creative-use-of-HUM score. If entries are still tied, one additional judge scores the tied entries on the same three criteria.

The judges' decisions are final.

8. Prizes

First place: $100. Second place: $50. Third place: $30. All three winners also receive HUM Pro for the rest of the winner's life.

Each winner's eligible Referrer receives the same prize as that winner (Section 6).

Cash is paid via Venmo, to the valid U.S. Venmo account the recipient gives when claiming.

Lifetime HUM Pro is added to the recipient's HUM account for good: it never ends, renews or bills. It works wherever the recipient signs in to HUM with that account, today on iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. To receive it, sign in to HUM with Apple or Google and send the account number HUM shows (it looks like HUM-XXXX-XXXX).

Prize value: each cash prize is worth its face value, and the Contest pays at most $360 in cash. HUM does not sell lifetime HUM Pro, so it has no set retail price. HUM Pro sells for $60 a year.

Prizes cannot be transferred or exchanged.

9. Winner Notification and Claims

On Wednesday, December 2, 2026, the Organizer emails each winner and each eligible Referrer from pranavnair963@gmail.com.

To claim, reply within seven (7) calendar days of that email and give, privately by email: a valid U.S. Venmo handle, your HUM account number for HUM Pro, and confirmation that you meet the rules in Section 3. The Organizer may ask for proof of your age and residence before awarding a prize, and for a tax form if the law requires one.

The Organizer never asks for a password, a bank or card number, or a fee of any kind. A message that asks for one does not come from the Organizer.

If a winner does not reply in time, is not eligible or cannot be verified, the placement passes to the next eligible entry in the judges' ranking, and that entrant and their Referrer follow the same steps.

For the winners' list, email the Organizer after December 2, 2026.

10. Taxes

Winners and Referrers are responsible for any taxes on their prizes. The Organizer issues any tax form the law requires and may ask for the details needed for it before paying.

11. Your Beat and Your Rights

Your beat must be your own original work, made entirely in HUM from your own hum. It must not copy another person's song, melody, lyrics or recording, and it must not infringe anyone's rights or break any law.

You keep ownership of your beat. By entering, you allow the Organizer and the judges to open, play and copy your beat only to judge and run the Contest.

Any public or marketing use of your beat, your name or your likeness needs your separate permission first. The Organizer will ask you before any such use.

Share with Friends makes a link that anyone who has it can open in HUM, so send your entry link only to the Contest's email address.

12. Disqualification

The Organizer can disqualify an entry, an entrant or a Referrer for fraud or an attempt to cheat; plagiarism, or a beat that is not original or not made in HUM; duplicate entries, including entries under more than one name or email address; fake, self- or ineligible referrals, or a Referrer added after the entry; or any other breach of these rules. A disqualified person receives no prize.

13. How Your Information Is Used

The Organizer uses what you send (your name, email address and beat link, your Referrer's name and email address, and when you claim, your Venmo handle, HUM account number and eligibility details) only to run the Contest: to judge entries, contact entrants and Referrers, check eligibility, pay cash prizes, grant HUM Pro, and meet legal duties.

Your information is not sold and is not added to any mailing list. It is shared only with the judges, with Venmo to make a payment, or where the law requires, and it is kept only as long as these purposes need. Email the Organizer to see or delete it. HUM's Privacy Policy covers the app itself.

If fraud, a technical failure or another cause outside the Organizer's control stops the Contest from running fairly, the Organizer may pause or end it and judge the eligible entries received before then. Any change is posted on this page.
