Get help

Email creator@hummusic.app with what happened, your iPhone model and iOS version, and a screenshot if you can. We answer every message.

Common questions

HUM doesn't hear me. Check that HUM may use the microphone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › HUM. Hum close to the phone in a quiet room.

"Can't reach HUM". HUM turns your hum into notes on its servers, so recording needs an internet connection. Your saved hums still open, play and edit offline.

Moving to a new phone. In the menu, tap "Export", choose "HUM Backup" with every HUM picked and save the file. On the new phone, tap "Import" in the menu and pick it.
