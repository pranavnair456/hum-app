Support

HUM – Voice to Notes

Get help

In the app, open the menu and tap "Report issues / Send feedback". Your report reaches us with the details we need to reproduce the problem. You can also email pranavnair2002@gmail.com.

Common questions

HUM doesn't hear me. Check that HUM may use the microphone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › HUM. Hum close to the phone in a quiet room.

"Can't reach HUM". HUM turns your hum into notes on its servers, so recording needs an internet connection. Your saved hums still open, play and edit offline.

Moving to a new phone. In My Hums, tap "Export my hums" and save the file. On the new phone, tap "Import hums" and pick it.
