Common Questions

HUM doesn't hear me. Check that HUM may use the microphone: Settings › Privacy & Security › Microphone › HUM. Hum close to the phone in a quiet room.

"Can't reach HUM". HUM turns your hum into notes with or without a connection. Your saved hums open, play and edit offline too; sharing needs a connection.
